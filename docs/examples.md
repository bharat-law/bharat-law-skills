# Three ways to use Bharat Law in Claude

These examples show requests and output shapes, not findings about a particular authority or court matter. Source links, findings and dates must come from the actual tool results.

## Verify a legal answer

**Provide:** the answer to check, your court or jurisdiction, and any relevant legal date. Include the material statutory subsections and penalty claims.

**Ask:** “Verify this Indian legal answer for my stated court. Check every relevant statutory subsection and distinguish binding from persuasive authorities. Lead with corrections and link the evidence.”

**Expect:** a short correction summary followed by a claim assessment with source links. For example, a penalty claim may be corrected only after the applicable subsection is read; missing facts remain unresolved. If you also request a Bharat Law research answer, the skill compares both answers against available source evidence and can correct either one. Research saves work and may use credits; verification lookup does not save a search.

## Check a citation and quotation

**Provide:** the citation or party names, court and date if known, plus the quotation or proposition attributed to it.

**Ask:** “Does this citation identify the right Indian judgment, and does its text support the proposition beside it? Check the quotation separately and link the authority.”

**Expect:** separate identity, quotation and proposition findings. An illustrative outcome is “Identity matched; quotation: Insufficient evidence — only a judgment summary was returned.” A matched citation alone does not verify its attributed holding, an exact quotation, or current good-law status. A failed lookup is reported as not found on Bharat Law, not proof of fabrication.

## Find a recorded next hearing

**Provide:** a case number or CNR if available, parties, court or bench, and the intended workspace when relevant.

**Ask:** “Find the recorded next hearing for my subscribed matter. Ask me to choose if several matters match. Show the recorded date, source freshness and when you checked.”

**Expect:** the identified matter, recorded listing, source update time when available, and check time. Multiple matches require selection. If no date is recorded, the result says so; it does not establish that no hearing is scheduled. Wider or fresh retrieval requires your intent and may use credits. Latest-order retrieval is not currently available.

## Continue your work

For related follow-ups, ask Claude to continue the same saved Bharat Law conversation. Research and drafts follow workspace and case permissions; switching workspace also changes the selection in Bharat Law web and mobile apps. Review generated answers and drafts before relying on them, filing or sharing them.
