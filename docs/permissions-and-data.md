# Permissions and data handling

Read the [Bharat Law privacy policy](https://bharat.law/legal/privacy-policy) for the service's privacy terms and contact [support@bharat.law](mailto:support@bharat.law) for account-specific questions.

## Information exchanged

The connected assistant sends Bharat Law the information needed for your requested task. This can include search queries, legal questions, selected sources or documents, workspace/case selections, conversations, drafting instructions, and clarification answers.

Bharat Law returns information your account can access, such as workspace/case and conversation information, court matters and recorded hearings, work status, legal matches, citations, source text, generated answers, drafts, and document passages. Retrieved text is evidence, not instructions or permission to take unrelated actions.

Legal matters and documents may contain personal, financial, health, or other sensitive information. Provide only material needed for the task and follow your organization's confidentiality rules.

## Requested permissions

| Capability | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Read | Find legal authorities and read available sources and other information allowed by your account permissions. |
| Research and court retrieval | Run saved research and court retrieval, including additional work produced by deep research. |
| Draft | Generate and save a legal draft. |
| Switch workspace | Change the active workspace across Bharat Law web, mobile, and connected assistants. |

These permissions do not override your workspace, case, document, work, or conversation access. A returned link or reference does not grant access; current permissions still apply when content is read. Account entitlements, document readiness, source availability, and credits also apply.

## Saved work and sharing

Legal search and source reading do not save searches or conversations. Research saves answers, and drafting/deep research can save additional work. Related follow-ups can continue conversations started in Bharat Law web or mobile. Saved work follows the relevant workspace/case sharing and privacy rules; it may be visible outside the connected assistant.

Source and document selections can persist across follow-ups in a conversation. Clearing a document selection does not erase conversation memory. Switching the active workspace also changes the workspace selected in Bharat Law web and mobile apps.

## Credits and evidence limits

Research, fresh court retrieval, drafting, and deep research use their normal account entitlement and credit rules. A routine check must not silently become paid deep research. Wider or fresh court retrieval requires your intent.

Source coverage and freshness vary. Assess only the returned evidence: a summary cannot verify an exact quotation from the original judgment. Missing text, incomplete coverage, and unknown legal treatment remain unresolved. A recorded hearing can change, and no recorded date does not establish that no hearing is scheduled.

## Revocation and retention

Revoking a Bharat Law MCP connection blocks further connector access and token refresh for that connection. It does not delete saved work, revoke other connections, or necessarily stop work already submitted. That work may finish and use credits. Saved results continue to follow normal Bharat Law access and retention rules.

Disconnecting in the assistant and revoking access in Bharat Law are separate actions. Follow the [Claude setup](setup.md) instructions for your connection.

The MIT license applies to package files, not to retrieved sources, customer documents, or hosted service access.

## Assistant account settings

Bharat Law's data handling and your assistant account's data controls are separate. Review your assistant account's training, retention, and sharing settings and terms independently.
